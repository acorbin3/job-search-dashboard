New tab

When you hit ↻ Refresh data on a tab's dashboard, each enabled source is searched and a job is kept only if its title matches a Level pattern and a Domain pattern, matches no Exclude pattern, and its description contains at least the given number of AI keywords. One regular expression per line, case-insensitive.

Resume & fit scoring

Each uploaded resume becomes a fit profile: a table of title levels, skill keywords, and company sectors that scores every job 0–100. The default profile scores every tab unless a tab pins a different one (the "Fit resume" dropdown above). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you pick the Claude method.
